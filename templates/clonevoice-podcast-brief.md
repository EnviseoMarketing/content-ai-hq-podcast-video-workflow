## Required output contract

1. Preserve every speaker label.
2. Export or make available **one audio file per labeled segment**, using `ALEX_001.wav`, `MAYA_001.wav`, `JACK_001.wav`, and so on.
3. Do not merge all speakers into a single unlabelled audio file.
4. Leave natural but short pauses at speaker handoffs; do not add long beds of silence.
5. Use a consistent voice for each recurring host across the episode.
6. Do not add music, applause, sound effects, or an intro unless explicitly requested in the episode brief.
7. Do not substitute another voice when an assigned voice is temporarily unavailable; stop and report the missing assignment.

## Conversation style

This is a conversation, not a sequence of essays. Use reactions, small interruptions, follow-up questions, and respectful disagreement. Alex keeps the topic moving; Maya explains the opportunity; Jack asks whether it actually saves time or makes money.
